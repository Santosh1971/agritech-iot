# AWD field node v0.3: segmented capacitive level strip on a 2-layer board

This board is the paddy AWD (alternate wetting and drying) field node. It is a T-shaped, **2-layer** PCB (v0.1 was 4 layers).

- **Head:** 40 × 60 mm, inside a sealed 50 mm PVC end cap.
  - **Front:** ESP32-S3-MINI-1, ISC-SX1262-B LoRa module (same module and pin map as WPC), SMA, the touch series resistors.
  - **Back:** power: battery connector J3 (LiFePO4 14500), magnetic-socket connector J4, charger, LDO, fuse, reverse protection, USB ESD.
- **Strip:** 16 × 300 mm, pushed into the AWD tube.
  - 12 sensing pads, 6 × 18 mm on a 20 mm pitch, covering +7 to −17 cm, plus a dry reference pad (REF) above them.
  - One 12 × 20 mm GND water-return pad at the bottom.

The node reports to a WPC-style Master over LoRa at 866 MHz.

## Why 2 layers works for the strip

| Layer | What is on it |
|---|---|
| F.Cu | Pads in the left column. On the right, a **staircase** of 0.15 mm sense lanes, each between two 0.15 mm **GND guard traces** (0.15 mm gaps). Each lane runs straight down and steps left into its pad; the guard on its left ends just above that pad in a via to the back GND. |
| B.Cu | GND: 25 % hatch behind the pads, solid behind the lanes. No routing allowed on the back of the strip. |

- **Water rises from the bottom, and every lane runs only above its own pad.** A pad is always wet before any part of its own lane is, so water touching a lane can only make an already-wet pad read wetter.
- **The guards stop crosstalk** between a wet lower pad's lane and a dry higher pad's lane, the job the inner GND planes did in v0.1.
- **Cost:** a 2-layer board, HASL finish and no impedance control costs roughly a third to a half of the 4-layer ENIG board (estimate, check the quote).
- **To verify on the bench before the field:** in a water tube, check that a dry pad's reading does not move when the pads below it go under water.

The v0.1 4-layer board and its Gerbers are kept in `archive/v0.1-4layer/` for comparison.

### Changes from v0.1/v0.2

- 4 layers → 2 layers; strip 10 → 16 mm; pads 8 → 6 mm wide; ENIG → HASL.
- **The touch bus is drawn by the generator, not the autorouter:** each ESP32 touch pin goes through a via to B.Cu, runs as a ladder straight down under the LoRa module (F.Cu above it is a solid GND pour), and steps back up into its 510 Ω resistor. Channels follow the pads, so nothing crosses: **REF = T13/IO13, P1 = T12/IO12 … P12 = T1/IO1. Firmware must use this map.**
- **SMA J1:** v0.1 placed it with its pads running 1.4 mm past the board edge. In the footprint the connector body face is where the pads end (2.54 mm from the origin), so v0.3 puts the pad ends 0.3 mm inside the edge.
- The power block moved to the back so the 13 touch traces have the front-left corner of the head to themselves.
- No top-layer tracks under the LoRa module: F.Cu there is solid GND under the radio, and only the touch bus passes, on B.Cu below that pour. The battery connector J3 moved to the back-right corner to make room. No tracks or vias within 0.6 mm of the head edges.
- Antenna feed: 1.0 mm grounded coplanar line, 0.2 mm gap to the F.Cu pour, solid GND behind. It is about 11 mm long (λ/16 at 866 MHz in FR4), so no impedance control is ordered.

## Pin map (ESP32-S3)

| Function | GPIO |
|---|---|
| Touch T13 → REF pad | IO13 |
| Touch T12 → P1, T11 → P2 … T1 → P12 (Pn = IO(13 − n)) | IO12 … IO1 |
| VBUS_DET (charger present, deep-sleep wake), to TP1 | IO14 |
| VBAT_ADC (ADC2_CH4) / VBAT_EN | IO15 / IO16 |
| LoRa DIO1 / BUSY / RST | IO17 / IO18 / IO21 |
| LoRa NSS / SCK / MOSI / MISO | IO39 / IO40 / IO41 / IO42 |
| LED | IO38 |
| PAIR/BOOT button | IO0 |
| USB D−/D+ and UART0 | J2 (1.27 mm pogo/header) |

## v0.2 power: charging in the field through a magnetic socket

v0.1 put the 14500 straight onto the 3V3 rail with no charger, so the box had to be opened to charge the cell. v0.2 adds the same charging block as TH2:

```
J4 magnetic socket (VBUS, D-, D+, GND) -> F1 0.5 A polyfuse -> VBUS -> U3 CN3058E (0.31 A, 3.6 V CV) -> VBAT
J3 14500 -> Q1 reverse protection -> VBAT -> U4 RT9080-33 (2 uA Iq) -> +3V3
```

- **The LDO is now required:** while charging, VBAT sits at 3.6 V, which is the ESP32-S3's absolute maximum.
- **Battery sense:** R4 now measures **VBAT** (before the LDO), not +3V3.
- **USB:** J4's D−/D+ join the native-USB lines already on J2, through U5 (USBLC6 ESD protection). The node can be flashed through the magnetic cable without opening the box.
- **Charge LEDs:** D2 red (charging) and D3 green (full) run off VBUS. Place them under, or next to, the front-shell light window.
- **Charger detect:** VBUS_DET (R12/R13 divider to IO14, an RTC GPIO) wakes the S3 when the cable is clipped on.
- **Enclosure:** the magnetic socket needs its own wall hole, because the side hole is used by the SMA. That waits until the socket's flange size is known (ElectronifyIndia 4-pin flanged for prototypes, HytePro M826 waterproof for production). It goes in the shared AWD/TH shell.

## Regenerate

Everything is generated from `tools/design.py` (parts, nets, strip geometry).

```bash
cd products/AWD1-paddy/hardware/AWD-FieldNode
zsh tools/build.sh      # schematic + ERC, board, Freerouting (retried until DRC is clean), stitching, silkscreen
zsh tools/fab.sh        # fab/: gerbers, drills, BOM, CPL, PDFs, STEP, renders
```

`build.sh` rebuilds the board from scratch every time, so any hand edits in KiCad are lost: make changes in `design.py` / `gen_pcb.py` instead. Freerouting 1.9.0 is expected at `~/Applications/freerouting/freerouting-1.9.0.jar`.

## Fab notes

- **2 layers, 1.6 mm FR4, HASL lead-free, green mask both sides, vias tented.** Minimum track/gap 0.15 mm, minimum drill 0.3 mm.
- Parts on **both sides**: power block on the back. For JLC assembly choose two-sided, or hand-solder the back.
- The pads have **no mask opening**. Below the neck the strip carries no exposed copper.
- After assembly, dip-coat the strip in 2-part epoxy or PU, 100–200 µm thick, and seal the routed edges.
