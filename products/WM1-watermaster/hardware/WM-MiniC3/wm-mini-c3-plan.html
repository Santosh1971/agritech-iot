WM-Mini C3

A larger FG1: one ESP32-C3 Super Mini switches a pump, a doser and 4 valves, scheduled by time or by litres. Rev A schematic and PCB are generated, checked and ready for review. All cables, USB included, leave from the bottom edge. Two boards fit one 99 × 98 panel.

For review: nothing ordered, nothing committed

What changed from the 0.3 plan

  1. Mirrored left to right. On an upright SRD the NO pin comes out on the left. With the 230 V corner on the left, the pump and doser NO and COM run straight down into J2 and J3 without crossing, and stay 5 mm from the valve header. The Super Mini, USB, flow input and LED row are now on the right.
  2. Super Mini pushed down to the edge. Its pin row is 1.3 mm from the bottom edge, which is as far as copper rules allow. Its antenna end still reaches over the right M3 screw head by about 1 mm, so screw the board down first, then plug in the module.
  3. Pluggable headers are the stackable 2EDGR type, body n × 5.08 mm. KiCad's Phoenix MSTBA has side flanges that did not fit. Same pitch and drills, with round 2.6 mm pads.
  4. Doser stays 230 V, as you said.
  5. 28 Oct, from your review:
    • ULN2003 and relay LEDs moved top-left; RTC, CR2032 and FLOW LED moved top-right.
    • C1 is now 1000 µF / 16 V.
    • The crystal is the through-hole C26.
    • The flow TVS is SMAJ5.0A (SMA package).
    • +5 V tracks are 1 mm.

Placement

  • Bottom left, 230 V: K1 PUMP and K2 DOSER upright, directly above J2 and J3. "230V~ KEEP CLEAR" is on the silk.
  • Top left, above the relays they drive: the ULN2003, the six relay LEDs (P D V1 V2 V3 V4) with their resistors, and C1, 1000 µF / 16 V. Everything here is more than 5 mm from 230 V.
  • Middle, valves: K3 V1 and K4 V2 are inverted at the top edge; K5 V3 and K6 V4 are upright below them. All coils meet near the centre line.
  • Top right, next to the ESP: the CR2032 standing vertical, the DS1307 with a through-hole C26 crystal and the pull-ups, the FLOW LED and TP1.
  • Bottom right: the Super Mini, with USB-C at the edge. Under it sit the flow-input parts (B5819W, SMAJ5.0A, 10k) and the IO2 pull-up.
  • Bottom edge, left to right: J2 PUMP (NO, COM), J3 DOSER (NO, COM), J4 VALVES (COM, V1–V4), J1 FLOW (5V, GND, SIG), USB-C.
  • Holes: 2 × M3, 92 mm apart on the centre line, as on the ESP32 test jig.

Schematic

A3 · fab/WM-MiniC3-schematic.pdf
  • Four blocks: MCU and power, real-time clock, flow input, relay driver and outputs.
  • Wired inside each block: the +5 V rail, R + LED across each coil, contacts to their headers, the valve COM bus, crystal, battery and pull-ups, and the flow chain. Net labels are used only between blocks (GPIO to ULN, ULN to coil, I2C).
  • Super Mini symbol grouped by function: power on top and bottom, relay drives and flow on the right, I2C and strapping pins on the left. IO8 and IO9 are marked as the on-module LED and BOOT button.

Bottom and panel

order the panel

Panel: 99 × 98 mm, a 2 mm routed slot with 3 plain tabs (no V-cut, no mouse bites). The top board is turned 180°, so both header edges face outwards and the overhanging plugs never sit over the other board.

Check before ordering

need a real part in hand
  1. 1:1 print. Print fab/WM-MiniC3-print-1to1.pdf at 100 %. Lay on it an SRD relay, a 2EDGR header, the CR2032 holder and the Super Mini; IO5 and 5V must be at the USB end.
  2. SRD NO pin: pad 3 = NO, as on WM1. Check one Rajiv RERL-32 with a meter.
  3. CR2032 holder: the footprint is the Mini-C3 one, and its − leg position is provisional. Measure the RBH-2.
  4. C1 size: the footprint is 10 mm diameter, 5 mm pitch. Check your 1000 µF part.
  5. Plug overhang: the plugs stick out about 8 mm past the bottom edge. Check this against the box wall.
