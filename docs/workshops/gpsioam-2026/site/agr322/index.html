GPS Institute of Agricultural Management · Farm IoT workshop

From studying IoT to building it

A hands-on day on building IoT for farms. You learn how any IoT system works (inputs, a controller, outputs and feedback), program a real ESP32 step by step, and take it from a blinking LED to Bluetooth, WiFi and the internet, with your own app on your phone. No electronics or programming background needed: you tell an AI assistant in plain English what you want. We also talk about why temperature and humidity matter on a farm, and what it takes to turn a farm idea into a business.

What we cover

Six things, one working system

1 · Starting an agritech business

The questions every founder has to answer before building anything: whose problem, who pays, how much, how you reach farmers, and who installs and services it.

2 · Why temperature and humidity matter

Crops, pest and disease alerts, greenhouses and cold storage: what the numbers tell a farmer, and what he does about them.

3 · How an IoT system works

Input → controller → output, with feedback. Sensors and actuators, digital and analogue signals, and a real-time clock (RTC) that keeps the time without internet.

4 · The controller: ESP32

Its inputs and outputs, and how sensors, switches, a relay and a clock connect to it.

5 · Firmware, step by step

Blink an LED → read a switch → temperature and humidity → water flow and a pump that protects itself → Bluetooth to your phone → WiFi → the internet (MQTT) → your own app.

6 · LoRa: long range without internet

Live demo by Avinash: our Wireless Pump Controller, one master switching two pump nodes across the campus.

You told us

What we learned from your answers

8 of 10 of you come from farming families, and 8 of 10 want to start your own business. Farmers lose most to pests and disease, and 7 of 10 of you said farmers spray only after they see the disease. Pumps run dry, burn out and get stolen. Farmers trust input dealers and neighbours, and believe what they see working on a real farm. Thank you for the honest answers: the day is built around them.
