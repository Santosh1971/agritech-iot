AGR 322 · Contemporary Agriculture · IoT workshop

From studying IoT to building it

You have studied wireless sensors, monitoring systems, IoT devices, plant health, soil moisture, tractors and drones, and seen a commercial system at Fyllo. On Thursday you build small versions yourself: program real farm devices, see live data, and make your own phone app. No electronics or programming background needed; you tell an AI assistant in plain English what you want, and use your agriculture knowledge to decide what the device should do.

Outcomes

After the workshop, students can

Do

  • Program a farm IoT device and read live sensor data
  • Use the device from a phone, in the field and over the internet
  • Retrieve logged data and turn it into a report
  • Describe a farm problem clearly enough for an AI assistant to build a working app

Explain

  • How temperature and humidity drive disease, pest, irrigation, spraying and storage decisions
  • Why pump control needs flow feedback
  • When to choose WiFi, LoRa, a mobile network or Bluetooth on a farm
  • Why devices store data when the network drops

Take home

  • A TH monitor unit, programmed by the student
  • Their app (Android APK or browser version)
  • Lab sheets and prompt cards at agrisenseandcontrol.in/workshop
  • Project ideas for the practical record
Logistics

What we bring, and what the college arranges

We bring

  • All hardware: TH monitor units, ESP32/ESP8266 boards, sensors, relays, a closed bulb box for the pump project, cables
  • Our field products for demonstration, a WiFi router and a projector-ready laptop
  • All software, lab sheets and apps

College arranges

  • A room with power sockets for 6 teams, and a projector
  • Permission for student laptops to install a USB driver if needed

Safety: students work only with USB and 5 V circuits. The 230 V bulb used as a pump stand-in is pre-wired in a closed box and handled only by the facilitators.
