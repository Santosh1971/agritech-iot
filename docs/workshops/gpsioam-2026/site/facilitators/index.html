For the host

Introductions to read out

Avinash Jha · about 1 minute

Please welcome Mr. Avinash Jha, CEO of AgriSense and Control, and an IoT engineer with more than five years of hands-on experience building, testing and deploying connected devices.

Mr. Avinash started his career at Sasya Systems in Bengaluru on smart-farm and cold-storage automation, where he assembled IoT circuit boards, connected them to the cloud, tested the apps and supported installations at customer sites. He then worked as an Embedded and IoT Engineer at Amazon India, as a Technical Consultant at Tinymesh on RF, BLE and GPRS wireless modules, and is now a Senior Quality Assurance Engineer at VOLANSYS, an ACL Digital company.

He brings a practical view of how sensors, wireless networks, cloud platforms and mobile apps come together in a working system, and what it takes to support it for real users. Please join me in welcoming Mr. Avinash Jha.

Santosh Kumar Jha · about 1 minute

Please welcome Mr. Santosh Kumar Jha, CTO of AgriSense and Control, and an electronics engineer with more than 30 years of experience building embedded systems and connected devices.

Mr. Jha studied Electronics Engineering at MANIT Bhopal and holds a Master of Engineering from the University of Michigan. He began his career designing microcontroller-based instruments and defence electronics, then led telematics and R&D work at ACTIA and General Motors. As Director of the Autocop Excellence Center, he built a 32-member R&D team whose vehicle tracking systems went into more than 45,000 vehicles.

In 2015 he brought this expertise to farming, founding A S Agri Systems, which developed the Nirantar Dhaara smart irrigation and fertigation systems; he has been a Partner at Sasya Systems since 2019. Today he designs AgriSense and Control’s wireless pump controllers, water-flow meters, drip-irrigation controllers and crop-climate monitors, which run on working farms. He is here to show how you can build real farm technology yourselves, with the help of AI, even without a coding background. Please join me in welcoming Mr. Santosh Kumar Jha.
