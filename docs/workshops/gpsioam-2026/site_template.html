Farm IoT Labs

Four hands-on ESP32 labs from our farm IoT workshop. Run every lab free in the Wokwi simulator in your browser, then build it on a real board with the same code.

1 · Simulate

Wokwi runs an ESP32 with sensors in your browser. No install, no hardware. A free account lets you save your work.

2 · Real board

Arduino IDE 2 + the Espressif “esp32” board package. Board: ESP32 Dev Module. Serial Monitor at 115200.

3 · Library

Labs 1, 3 and 4 need DHT sensor library for ESPx (Library Manager).

Lab 4

A phone dashboard, built with AI

Unit 4 · Smartphone apps

Ask an AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini) to write a web dashboard that your ESP32 serves over its own WiFi. Your job is to give it exact details, test what it writes, and paste errors back until it works. This lab needs a real board: a phone cannot join a simulated ESP32’s WiFi.

The loop

  1. Describe the board, pins, sensors and goal
  2. Generate code, wiring and an explanation
  3. Compile it (or paste it into Wokwi to check it builds)
  4. Flash the real board
  5. Paste back the exact error or Serial output
  6. Understand: ask about any line you can’t explain

Then

  • Join WiFi FarmNode-01 from your phone and open 192.168.4.1
  • Ask for one new feature at a time: an Auto mode, a threshold slider, a buzzer alarm
  • Never use AI advice to wire 230 V mains. That is work for a licensed electrician
