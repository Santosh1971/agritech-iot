Privacy Policy

NB Agri Automation — Last updated: August 23, 2026

This app is used to monitor and control NB Agri Automation's irrigation devices (pump schedules, water delivery, and device status). This policy explains what information the app handles and how it's used.

No account or personal sign-up

The app does not require creating an account, and does not ask for your name, email, or any personal identity information. You connect the app to your device by entering the device's ID.

What information the app handles

  • Device telemetry — pump on/off status, water delivered, cycle schedules, WiFi signal strength, and similar operational data from your irrigation device. This is used only to show you the device's status and history within the app.
  • WiFi credentials (setup only) — when you connect a device to your home/farm WiFi network during setup, those credentials are sent directly from your phone to the device itself over a local, direct connection. They are not sent to or stored on any server.

Where data is sent

Device telemetry is sent to NB Agri Automation's own server infrastructure to enable remote monitoring and control of your device. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or provided to any third party for marketing purposes.

No ads or third-party tracking

This app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking software development kits (SDKs).

Local network access

The app may request permission to access devices on your local WiFi network. This is used solely to connect directly to your irrigation device during setup and while it's on the same network — not to access any other device on your network.

Data retention

Device telemetry is retained on our servers to provide you with device history and status. You can request deletion of your device's data at any time by contacting us using the details below.
